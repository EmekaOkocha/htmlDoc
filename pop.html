🎨 Pop Ceiling and Design Services

Service Scope and Materials

We provide professional **installation services** for pop ceilings using **high-standard, premium materials**. We also offer **design consultation** to help clients select the perfect aesthetic.

Quotation Calculation (1-Hour Estimate)

The quotation is determined by a combination of two factors:

  1. Measurement: The price is calculated per **square meter (sqm)** of the area.
  2. Design Complexity: Highly intricate or bespoke designs will incur an additional charge.

➡️ Information Required for Initial Quote

To provide a preliminary estimate within 1 hour, please submit the following:

  • Dimensions (length and width) of the room(s).
  • Pictures of the current ceiling and the overall room space.
  • Room Height (from floor to existing ceiling/joist).

⚠️ **MANDATORY NOTE:** While we provide an estimate, a **mandatory on-site survey** is required to confirm all measurements and ensure the final quote is accurate.
