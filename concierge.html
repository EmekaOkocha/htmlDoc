✈️ Concierge Services: Travel Reservations & Tours

Travel Reservation Scope

We specialize in securing **local and international flight reservations only**. We do not currently handle reservations for rail, bus, or other ground transport methods.

Specialized Tour Offerings

We offer highly specialized tour packages tailored for:

  • Business Travel: Streamlined logistics and accommodation support.
  • Major Sporting Events: Packages for international events like the World Cup, Nations Cup, and similar high-profile sports competitions.

Pricing and Quotation

Pricing for specialized tours (Business, Events) is **variable** and depends on the event, duration, location, and included services. We **do not** have a flat rate.

➡️ Information Required for Flight Reservations (1-Hour Quote)

To initiate a flight booking and receive a quote within 1 hour, please provide the following mandatory details:

  • Full Name(s) of all travelers (as they appear on the passport/ID).
  • Date of Travel (Departure and Return).
  • Number of People traveling.
  • Class of Plane preference (Economy, Business Class, or First Class).
