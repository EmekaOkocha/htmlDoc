🛰️ Car Tracking System

State-of-the-art vehicle security, real-time monitoring, and remote immobilization.

Key Features

Our Car Tracking System offers crucial security and monitoring features for peace of mind:

  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor your vehicle's exact location, movement, and status 24/7 via GPS technology. [Image of a car location being displayed on a mobile GPS map interface]
  • Engine Cut-Off (Immobilization): The crucial ability to remotely turn off the engine via the mobile application in case of theft or unauthorized use.
  • Mobile Application Access: Manage and monitor your vehicle directly from your smartphone with user-friendly controls.

Fees and Subscription

The cost structure involves an **initial installation fee** and a **recurring service subscription** fee.

  • The **Service Subscription Fee** (paid monthly or yearly) is required to keep the tracking service and mobile app active and running.
  • The specific cost for the installation and the subscription rates will be provided in your official quotation (within 1 hour of request).

Warranty

We provide a **3-month warranty** covering the tracking device hardware and the installation workmanship, effective from the date of installation.
